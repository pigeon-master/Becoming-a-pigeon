# Red compact-car visitor

The car button sits between the dog and human icons. The visitor is 2.5 times its original size and uses at most one round trip on a broad track: curved passes through the flock connect to distant turnarounds, 40 units from the center. The camera initially zooms out only enough to fit the vehicle near the flock, then stays fixed throughout driving and the exit. It never follows the vehicle or changes zoom during exit. The original view is restored after departure. The central passes remain visible while the distant turnarounds use the broader plaza. The first pass starts behind the flock relative to the camera, keeping collisions in front of the car. The exit begins from the current position and heading, follows a short circular turn of radius 12 toward the scene, and continues straight away. It never returns to a fixed foreground waypoint. Exit speed smoothly eases from 55.65 to 44.52 over the first 24 units, then holds the slower speed to allow time to read the rear lettering. The car fades as its projected size becomes small. Track speed is 55.65; entry speed is 59.85 (both increased by 5%). Only physical contact with the scaled vehicle footprint launches pigeons. Contact is sampled at intervals of at most 10 ms. 90% of first impacts use a stronger launch (forward 31.5 to 40.95, upward 16.8 to 21, drag 0.75) to send more birds offscreen. The remaining 10% use forward 18.9 to 25.2, upward 13.65 to 16.8 and drag 1.35 to retain visible landings and repeat contact. Launches vary in direction, usually scattering toward the camera. Each pigeon has randomized rotation on three axes, damped over time, uneven wingbeats and an upright recovery before landing after about 2.3 to 3.5 seconds. Visible landings lie on their side, stay still for 1.2 to 2.1 seconds, then stand up and resume normal walking. The next hit always uses the stronger launch; its horizontal impulse is increased if necessary so its landing lies outside the view. After one lap the car makes additional curved approaches to any remaining pigeons. A strong launch counts as cleared after 0.2 seconds, without waiting for its offscreen landing. It starts its exit once at most three original participants remain (at least one must have left for a small flock), skipping the remainder of the route. Exit is latched so returning pigeons never restart the chase. Pigeons that land outside the view wait offscreen. Once the car is at least 75 units from the flock center and its visible rear plate shrinks below 40 screen pixels wide, those birds begin re-entering in randomized walk/fly batches using the same src/visitor-return.ts functions as the human and dog. Walking uses the normal head bob, leg gait and collision steering; flight uses flapping wings and a landing arc. Stored pigeons and speech are never deleted. Unhit pigeons keep walking without an alarm chain.

`src/compact-car.ts` builds one rounded photo-projected shell, opaque windows, rotating wheels, the exact rear plate `99구9999`, and a white `보초` decal on the rear-view left. The Gungsuh lettering was rendered to `public/models/car-rear-lettering.png` on the development computer, so its appearance does not depend on fonts installed on a visitor's device. `src/car-visit.ts` handles the route, collisions and recovery.

The image is loaded only when the car button is first used. The built-in image_gen tool created `public/models/compact-car-atlas.png` using the user's attached car photos as references. Text is rendered separately in code so the plate and lettering remain exact.

## Final texture prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: square 2x2 orthographic photographic texture atlas for a low-poly red compact Korean hatchback 3D game car. Reference images are the THREE RED CAR PHOTOS at the end of the user's latest message, not dog or person: use their rounded tall tiny five-door Matiz / Morning hatchback shape and styling. Create ONE square image divided into four equal square panels with no gaps. Top left: EXACT straight front view of shiny red compact hatchback, opaque pitch-black windscreen (no interior visible), rounded headlights, grille and fog lights, NO front license plate at all and no text/logos. Top right: EXACT straight rear view of same hatchback, pitch black opaque rear window, tall taillights, red hatch, small blank pale rear license rectangle but no writing. Bottom left: EXACT side orthographic view of same car facing RIGHT, wheels visible, all windows opaque black, door seams and handles. Bottom right: seamless rich red glossy automotive paint texture with subtle realistic variation, no car, no features. Each car fills its panel bounding box with a small equal margin, no perspective, no cast shadow, neutral soft even studio lighting and flat medium red background close to paint. Matching scale, consistent design. No captions, no people, no other objects. Do NOT draw text; text is added in code.

## Validation

`npm.cmd run build` checks TypeScript and production bundling. `npm.cmd run test:human` includes browser assertions for vehicle contact, untouched birds, camera-facing rear exit, full recovery, and existing human/dog visitors.
